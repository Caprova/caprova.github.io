Record Snapshot for Salesforce: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

In short: Record Snapshot turns the Salesforce page you are viewing into a PDF entirely inside your browser. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. Nothing you capture is sent to us or to anyone else.

This policy describes the Record Snapshot for Salesforce browser extension (the "Extension"), published by Caprova ("we", "us").

What the Extension accesses

The Extension accesses Salesforce pages only when you start a capture. You start one by clicking Capture to PDF or using the keyboard shortcut. It also accesses the current Salesforce page when you open its popup, so it can list that page's tabs. During a capture it:

  • takes screenshots of the Salesforce page in your active tab;
  • reads the page title, web address, Salesforce object name and record ID, to label the PDF;
  • reads the names of the page's tabs, so you can choose which ones to include;
  • temporarily scrolls the page, switches tabs and expands collapsed sections, then restores them.

The Extension runs only on Salesforce web addresses (*.force.com, *.lightning.force.com, *.my.salesforce.com). It does not read other websites, your browsing history, or your Salesforce password or login session.

Where your data goes

All processing happens on your computer, inside your browser. The Extension has no server and makes no network requests to us or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code.

Settings set by your organization

If your employer manages your browser, their IT administrator may pre-set some Extension settings (for example, the default page size). Those settings come from your organization's browser policy and are not sent to us.

Our commitments

We do not sell or transfer your data, use it for advertising, or use it for any purpose unrelated to creating your PDF. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. The Extension's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Children

The Extension is a business tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes to this policy

We may add optional features in future that connect to your Salesforce organization, such as saving a PDF to the record. If we do, we will update this policy before the feature is released and describe exactly what is sent and where. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
