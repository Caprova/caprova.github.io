Record Snapshot Support

Need help? Email getcaprova@gmail.com. Include your browser (Chrome or Edge), the Salesforce object, and a screenshot of anything that looks wrong.

Install

Record Snapshot is available for Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Store links are coming soon. In the meantime, contact us for access.

Using it

  1. Open a record in Salesforce Lightning.
  2. Click the Record Snapshot icon in the toolbar. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so it stays visible.
  3. Choose This view to capture what's on screen, or All tabs and sections to capture every tab. In All tabs mode, tick the tabs you want. Your choice is remembered for that object.
  4. Click Capture to PDF. Keep the Salesforce tab in front while the page scrolls.
  5. A preview opens in a new tab. Click Download PDF to save it.

Shortcut: Alt+Shift+S captures the current view. Settings (page size, file name, tabs to skip) are under Settings in the popup.

Troubleshooting

"Open a Salesforce Lightning page first"

The extension works only on Salesforce Lightning pages (addresses ending in lightning.force.com or my.salesforce.com). Salesforce Classic isn't supported.

An error after updating the extension

Refresh the Salesforce tab and try again. Pages that were open before an update keep the old version until they're refreshed.

Part of the PDF is blank or shows another tab

Keep the Salesforce tab in front until the preview opens. Switching browser tabs during a capture interrupts it.

A tab I want is missing from the checklist

Tabs nested inside another tab are captured as currently shown, not separately. Open the inner tab you want before capturing.

Faint lines or a misaligned strip in the PDF

Try the High fidelity engine in Settings. It renders the whole page in one pass.

For IT administrators

You can force-install the extension and pre-set or lock its settings through browser policy (Google Admin, Microsoft Intune or Group Policy). Configurable settings: mode, engine, pageSize, autoDownload, fileNamePattern, skipTabs. Contact us for a deployment guide.
